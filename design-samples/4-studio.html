Week 11 · Bugs/Day 5

Writing a report that needs no follow-up

Write all seven sections Title a bug so it is findable Pass the no-questions test

This is the most valuable thing a tester produces. Not the bug — the report.

The standard is one sentence long: an engineer should be able to act on it without asking you anything.

The shape

Title       Applying the same discount code twice stacks
            the reduction (cart)

Environment Meridian sandbox, stage 3 · Chrome 131 · 7 Nov

Steps       1. Add Harmattan Field Jacket to the cart
            2. Enter SAVE10 and click Apply
            3. Enter SAVE10 again and click Apply

Expected    Refused; the total stays at NGN 43,650.00
Actual      Accepted; the total drops to NGN 38,800.00

Severity    Critical          Priority  Highest

Read it as an engineer: you know what to do, where to go, what you will see, and what it should have been.

The sentence people leave out

When severity and priority differ, say why. One line prevents the argument — without it, someone reads “Critical” and “Low” together and comes to ask.

Tone

You are describing a system, never a person. “Applying the same code twice stacks the reduction” gets fixed faster than “you broke the discount logic” — because a report that reads as an accusation invites a defence, and a defence is a delay.

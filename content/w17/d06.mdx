Six tickets. For each: the diagnosis, the owning team, and whether it is yours to resolve. Two need escalations, and you will write them.

Work each one before reading the answer.

---

## Ticket 1

> **Merchant:** "Customers click Pay and nothing happens at all. No error, no spinner, nothing."

**Evidence:** Network panel empty on click. Console shows `Uncaught TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'value')` at `checkout.js:142`.

<details>
<summary>Diagnosis</summary>

**Front end.** No request left the browser, so nothing reached the API — do not search the server logs. A script error at line 142 killed the click handler before it could call anything. Probably an element the code expects is missing in this state.

**Owner:** front-end team. **Priority:** P1 if it affects all customers of this merchant; confirm the scope first.
</details>

---

## Ticket 2

> **Customer:** "I was charged but my order was never created."

**Evidence:** `POST /payments` returned `201`, reference `PAY-8841`, amount correct. Payment record shows `status=successful`. No order exists. Webhook log: three delivery attempts to the merchant's endpoint, all `502`.

<details>
<summary>Diagnosis</summary>

**Not a product bug.** The payment succeeded and we attempted delivery three times; the merchant's own endpoint returned `502` each time, so their system never learned about it.

**Owner:** the merchant's engineering team, with support holding the customer. **Action:** tell the merchant their endpoint is down and offer to re-send once it is back. The end customer needs the order created or the payment refunded today — that is the thing that cannot wait.

This is the succeeded-but-unknown shape from week 16, and it is most of the hard tickets in payments.
</details>

---

## Ticket 3

> **Customer:** "Your site says my card is invalid but it works everywhere else."

**Evidence:** `POST /payments` returned `422`, body `{"error":"validation_failed","fields":["card.number"],"message":"Card number failed checksum"}`. Screen shows "Something went wrong."

<details>
<summary>Diagnosis</summary>

**Two findings.** The server's rejection may well be correct — a checksum failure usually means a mistyped digit. But the page discarded a perfectly clear explanation and showed a generic message, so the customer cannot fix it.

**Owner:** front-end team for the message. **Your action now:** ask them to re-enter the number carefully, since a checksum failure is nearly always a typo. Then raise the message as a finding with a count if you have seen it before.
</details>

---

## Ticket 4

> **Merchant:** "Settlement for 6 November has not arrived. NGN 4,100,000."

**Evidence:** Payout record exists, `status=sent`, `sent_at=2026-11-06T16:40Z`, bank reference present. No failure anywhere.

<details>
<summary>Diagnosis</summary>

**Nothing broken on our side**, and that is not the same as nothing to do. We sent it with a bank reference; it is with their bank.

**Action:** give them the bank reference and the sent timestamp so their finance team can trace it, explain the posting schedule, and give a date by which it should appear. If it is past that date, escalate to the payments operations team to trace with the bank — **do not** leave the merchant to chase their bank alone with four million naira outstanding.

"Not our bug" is a diagnosis. It is not a resolution.
</details>

---

## Ticket 5

> **Partner developer:** "We stopped receiving webhooks at 02:00. No retries either."

**Evidence:** Delivery log shows attempts ceasing at 01:58. Endpoint registered and unchanged. Their endpoint responds `200` to a manual test now. Three other partners on the same delivery worker also stopped at 01:58.

<details>
<summary>Diagnosis</summary>

**Back end, and an incident.** Four partners on the same worker stopped within the same minute — that is not four coincidences, it is one stuck or dead delivery worker.

**Owner:** back-end or platform team. **Priority:** P1. Multiple partners have been silently missing events for hours, and every hour adds data they will have to reconcile.

**This one needs an escalation**, written below.
</details>

---

## Ticket 6

> **Customer:** "The transaction list only shows 50 rows. I have hundreds."

**Evidence:** `GET /transactions` returns `200` with 50 items and `"hasMore": true`. The page has no pagination controls.

<details>
<summary>Diagnosis</summary>

**Front end.** The API is paginating correctly and telling the client there is more; the page ignores `hasMore` and renders only the first page.

**Owner:** front-end team. **Priority:** P2 — the data is inaccessible, with a partial workaround through export if that works.

**Workaround to give today:** the API returns everything if they can call it, and the export may too. Check before offering it.
</details>

## Write the two escalations

### Ticket 5 — to the platform team

> **Webhook delivery stopped at 01:58 for at least four partners (P1).**
>
> Delivery attempts cease at 01:58 UTC for `lumen-pay`, `arcadia`, `flux-ng` and `beacon`, all on the same delivery worker. No retries have been attempted since. Partner endpoints are healthy — `lumen-pay` returns 200 to a manual test now.
>
> Scope: at least four partners, approximately 6 hours of undelivered events so far, growing. Each partner's system is now out of sync with ours and none of them knows it.
>
> Checked and ruled out: endpoint configuration unchanged, partner endpoints responding, no change on their side. Other workers appear unaffected.
>
> Partners have been told we are investigating, with an update due at 11:00.
>
> Needed: whether the worker is stuck or dead, and whether queued events will replay automatically or need manual re-delivery.

### Ticket 1 — to the front-end team

> **Pay button does nothing for merchant ACC-4421 (P1 pending scope).**
>
> `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'value')` at `checkout.js:142` fires on click. No network request is made, so nothing reaches the API and there is nothing in the server logs.
>
> Reproduced on a test account with the same configuration; see the attached console capture (redacted).
>
> Scope: confirming now whether this affects all of this merchant's customers or a subset. Treating as P1 until known.
>
> Merchant acknowledged, update due 11:00.

Both carry: the evidence, what was ruled out, the scope, what the customer has been told, and what is actually needed. Neither contains a guess.

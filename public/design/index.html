Tracesmith · design review

Five directions, one course.

The same week 11 lesson rendered five ways. Each one has a working home, course index and lesson page — the navigation switches between them, so click around.

The current site puts 41 bordered boxes on a single lesson and runs 6,157 pixels tall. Every direction below removes most of those boxes and uses the Cormorant Garamond, Jost and JetBrains Mono pairing from Python Atlas and Engram.
01 Atlas Your house style, applied faithfully. Deep navy, hairline rules instead of boxes, callouts as a flush-left accent rule. Open → 02 Paper The NobleThread register. Warm paper, a set book page, a drop cap, and callouts moved into the margin as notes. Open → 03 Focus A lesson as seven steps instead of one long scroll. One idea per screen; arrow keys work. The direct answer to “stressful”. Open → 04 Studio An application shell. Course tree always on the left, contents on the right, brass on near-black. You never lose your place. Open → 05 Field An engineer’s notebook. Faint drafting grid, mono metadata, blueprint blue, tick and cross marks. The most “QA” of the five. Open →

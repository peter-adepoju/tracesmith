Week 11 · Bugs/Day 5

Writing a report that needs no follow-up

By the end you can
  • Write all seven sections well
  • Title a bug so it is findable
  • Judge your own report against the no-questions test

This is the most valuable thing a tester produces. Not the bug — the report.

The standard is one sentence long: an engineer should be able to act on it without asking you anything.

The shape

Title       Applying the same discount code twice stacks
            the reduction (cart)

Environment Meridian sandbox, stage 3 · Chrome 131 · 7 Nov

Steps       1. Add Harmattan Field Jacket to the cart
            2. Open the cart
            3. Enter SAVE10 and click Apply
            4. Enter SAVE10 again and click Apply

Expected    The second application is refused; the total
            stays at NGN 43,650.00

Actual      The discount becomes 20%. The total drops to
            NGN 38,800.00. Ten applications reach NGN 0.00

Severity    Critical          Priority  Highest

Read it as an engineer: you know what to do, where to go, what you will see, and what it should have been. Nothing is missing, and you did not have to ask.

The title does most of the work

It is read in a list of a hundred, in a notification, in a stand-up. The pattern is what happens, where, and under what condition.

WeakStrong
Discount bugSame discount code applied twice stacks the reduction
Cart brokenCart total ignores quantity for items added twice
Payment issueDouble-clicking Place order creates two orders and charges twice
The sentence people leave out

When severity and priority differ, say why. One line, and it prevents the argument.

Without it, someone reads “Critical” and “Low” together and comes to ask. With it, the trade-off is visible and agreed.

Tone

You are describing a system, never a person.

AvoidPrefer
You broke the discount logicApplying the same code twice stacks the reduction
Obviously nobody tested thisNot covered by the existing cases; I have added DISC-003

The right-hand column gets fixed faster. Not because engineers are fragile, but because a report that reads as an accusation invites a defence, and a defence is a delay.

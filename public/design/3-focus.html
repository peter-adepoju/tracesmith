Week 11 · Bugs · Day 5
The lesson

Writing a report that needs no follow-up

This is the most valuable thing a tester produces. Not the bug — the report.

The standard is one sentence long: an engineer should be able to act on it without asking you anything.

Step 2 · The shape

Seven sections, every time

Title       Applying the same discount code twice
            stacks the reduction (cart)

Environment Meridian sandbox · Chrome 131 · 7 Nov

Steps       1. Add the jacket to the cart
            2. Enter SAVE10 and click Apply
            3. Enter SAVE10 again and click Apply

Expected    Refused. Total stays NGN 43,650.00
Actual      Accepted. Total drops to NGN 38,800.00

Severity    Critical      Priority   Highest
Step 3 · The title

It is read in a list of a hundred

The pattern: what happens, where, and under what condition.

Weak

  • Discount bug
  • Cart broken
  • Payment issue
  • Urgent!!

Strong

  • Same code applied twice stacks the reduction
  • Cart total ignores quantity for items added twice
  • Double-clicking Place order charges twice
  • POST /payments returns 500 above 1,000,000
Step 4 · The one people skip

When severity and priority differ, say why

“Critical severity, low priority: only two internal users reach it and exporting in two halves works.”

Without that line, someone reads the two ratings together, decides one of them is a mistake, and comes to ask. With it, the trade-off is visible and already agreed.

Step 5 · Tone

Describe the system, never the person

Avoid

  • You broke the discount logic
  • Obviously nobody tested this
  • Why does this keep happening?

Prefer

  • Applying the same code twice stacks the reduction
  • Not covered by existing cases; I have added DISC-003
  • Third discount defect this sprint — worth a look at that area?

The right-hand column gets fixed faster. Not because engineers are fragile, but because a report that reads as an accusation invites a defence, and a defence is a delay.

Step 6 · Before you file

Is there a credential in it?

A report that leaks a key is worse than no report at all. The bug would have waited; the exposure does not — the key now lives in a ticket system and its backups, and it has to be rotated.

Check the evidence for tokens, card numbers, passwords and session cookies before it leaves your hands.
